Control systems · Education · July 2025

BSc in Computer and Electronic Engineering, University of Pavia

I completed my BSc in Computer and Electronic Engineering at the University of Pavia with 110/110 cum laude, defending a thesis on event-triggered eco-driving for vehicle platoons in urban traffic.

My undergraduate thesis brought together control theory and energy-aware driving. The work studied event-triggered eco-driving for vehicle platoons moving through an urban traffic network, asking how coordination and control decisions can reduce energy use while respecting system constraints.

Follow-up work

The project was supervised by Professor A. Ferrara and Professor G. P. Incremona (Politecnico di Milano). It led to a first-author manuscript submitted to the 25th European Control Conference (ECC 2027), and it remains one of my project threads alongside medical robotics and scientific machine learning.

Completing the degree also marked the end of a rewarding period in Pavia, where engineering studies ran in parallel with the IUSS advanced studies program and life at Almo Collegio Borromeo.
