Robotics & bionics · June–September 2025

Medical robotics at the Max Planck Institute

As a Robotics Institute Germany intern at the Max Planck Institute for Intelligent Systems in Stuttgart, I worked on two medical-robotics projects.

In the Medical Systems group, I worked on coils for robotically assisted, on-demand hyperthermia for tissue ablation, and on a hydraulically actuated soft catheter for interventional procedures under MRI guidance.

Two routes to less invasive intervention

The projects explored how robotic systems and carefully designed devices can support targeted treatment and image-guided procedures. Working across actuation, device design and medical constraints made the internship a valuable introduction to research at the boundary of robotics and healthcare.

The experience also helped shape the questions I continue to pursue in bionics engineering: how to build compact, controllable systems that can operate safely in complex clinical environments.
