Research life · Computer science · August 2024

What a pre-doctoral research school taught me about research

At the Cornell, Maryland, Max Planck Pre-doctoral Research School in Computer Science in Saarbrücken, I explored research areas ranging from intermittent computing to high-performance computing.

The school offered a concentrated look at questions at the frontier of computer science. Lectures that stayed with me covered type systems for intermittent computing, cryptographic software, advanced network attacks and high-performance computing.

The people are part of the research

The most lasting part was the chance to meet professors, researchers and students from different places and backgrounds. Their perspectives helped me think more clearly about whether research and a future PhD might be the right path for me.

I left with new questions, new connections and a better sense of how broad computer science research can be.
