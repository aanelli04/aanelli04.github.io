Robotics & bionics · 2026–present

Miniaturized coils for local magnetic control

My current research engineer internship at Sant’Anna School of Advanced Studies focuses on local magnetic control strategies using miniaturized coils mounted on an endoscopic device.

The work sits within Medical Robotics at Sant’Anna and explores how small coils can generate useful local control for an endoscopic system. The broader engineering challenge is to make actuation compact enough for the device while retaining the ability to steer and control it precisely.

Research context

This research connects my Bionics Engineering studies with my earlier work on soft catheters, MRI-guided interventions and robotic medical systems. It is supervised by Professor V. Iacovacci. The project is ongoing, so this page will grow as methods and results become shareable.
