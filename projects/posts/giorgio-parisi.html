Meeting Nobel laureate Giorgio Parisi at Almo Collegio Borromeo

An invitation from Almo Collegio Borromeo gave me the chance to attend a conference with Professor Giorgio Parisi and join a small meeting for STEM students.

Professor Parisi spoke about the systems that shape the world, both in physics and in human life. The conference was followed by a private conversation with students, where we could ask questions and discuss scientific challenges directly.

Reflections

What stayed with me was not only the scientific perspective, but the value of having time to ask questions in an informal setting. It was a reminder that learning depends on curiosity, collaboration and the willingness to approach difficult ideas from different angles.

I am grateful to Professor Parisi for sharing his time and to Almo Collegio Borromeo for creating that opportunity.
