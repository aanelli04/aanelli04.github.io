Physics · Instrumentation · 2021–2023

From a student proposal to a DESY beamline: the ExTRa experiment

Our team won CERN Beamline for Schools in 2021 with ExTRa, an experiment to investigate transition radiation. We carried it out at DESY and later published the results.

The project started with a student proposal to measure the energy and angular distributions of transition-radiation X-rays. Winning the Beamline for Schools competition gave our team the opportunity to perform the experiment on a DESY beamline in Hamburg.

From beam time to analysis

After the test beam, I analysed ExTRa data with Python at INFN Bari. The collaboration continued beyond the beamline and resulted in a paper in the Journal of Instrumentation. I also presented the work at the 10th Beam Telescopes and Test Beams Workshop in Lecce.

ExTRa was my first sustained experience of experimental physics: a team-defined question, an instrument under real constraints and a data-analysis path from measurements to publication.
