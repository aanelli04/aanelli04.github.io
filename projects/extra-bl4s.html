EXTRA-BL4S
transition radiation at DESY

A student-led experiment measuring transition-radiation X-rays, carried out at the DESY II Test Beam facility.

The work

The experiment

Our EXTRA proposal was selected in the 2021 Beamline for Schools competition, organised by CERN and DESY. The team travelled to Hamburg to study transition radiation from fast electrons and positrons crossing different radiators.

The measurements used a silicon pixel detector and contributed to results published in the Journal of Instrumentation.
