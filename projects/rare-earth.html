The work

Combining spectral physics and machine learning

Rare earth elements are important to modern technologies. My IUSS diploma thesis brought together remote sensing, hyperspectral data analysis and software development in a user-friendly Python pipeline.

The research continues into hybrid approaches combining spectral knowledge with machine learning, aiming for detection that is reliable and easier to inspect.

Thesis: “Web-Enabled and User-Friendly Python Pipeline for Rare Earth Elements Mapping from Hyperspectral Imagery.”
