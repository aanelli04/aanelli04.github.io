The work

Engineering at the boundary of care.

My internship in the Medical Systems group involved designing coils for robotically assisted hyperthermia for tissue ablation, and developing a hydraulically actuated soft catheter for MRI-guided interventions.

The experience brought together mechanics, materials, control and imaging constraints. My current Sant’Anna research studies local magnetic control strategies using miniaturized coils mounted on an endoscopic device, supervised by Prof. V. Iacovacci.
